ACMAS301 - AC Service Station

ACMAS301 AC Service Station

Refrigerant Recovery, Recharge and Purifying Station

Works with R134a

DaTo ACMAS301 A/C Service Station is built for refrigerant recovering, recharging and purifying. Developed with durable components and reliable SCM control programs, this CE certified machine offers simple operation, LCD display, vehicle database and printer support for professional workshops.

  • Recovering and recycling with used oil discharge
  • Vacuum and vacuum leak test
  • New oil injection and refrigerant charging
  • Full automatic mode for recovery, vacuum, oil injection and charge
  • Unidirectional and bidirectional flush
  • A/C database included with USB update support
  • Thermal printer, condenser, cooling fan and heater band
