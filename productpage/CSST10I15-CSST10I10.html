Description

Product overview

Upgrade your workshop with our industrial screw air compressor—engineered for continuous operation, high efficiency, and long-term reliability. Designed for automotive workshops, tyre centres, body shops, and industrial use across UAE and the Middle East, it delivers powerful, stable compressed air with minimal vibration and noise.

Advanced oil-injected screw technology ensures smooth compression, reduced wear, and extended service life. The system is ideal wherever consistent airflow matters: garages, heavy vehicle workshops, detailing, tyre service, body & paint bays, and production units.

Applications

  • Tyre service centres
  • Heavy vehicle workshops
  • Car detailing centres

  • Body & paint shops
