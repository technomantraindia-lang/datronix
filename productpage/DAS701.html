Product Overview

  • Android tablet-based diagnostic tool with simple and intuitive navigation.
  • Supports over 100 car brands across Europe, America, Asia and China.
  • Powerful TPMS scanner tool suitable for more than 98% of vehicles equipped with tire pressure systems.
