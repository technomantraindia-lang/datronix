Description

Product Overview

  • Special inspection level EV battery inspection equipment for workshops and service centers.
  • Combines battery pack detection, detailed battery status and battery fault information.
  • Supports whole vehicle system diagnostics with code reading, clearing, data flow and action tests.
  • Automatically calculates total voltage, differential pressure and highest or lowest voltage data.
  • Supports four battery pack connection methods: OBD, special connector, jumper and fast charging port.
  • Equipped with professional battery pack connector and aviation plug connector for faster detection.
  • Dual Wi-Fi design keeps VCI communication independent while the host accesses the internet.
  • Supports expansion modules including insulation tester, oscilloscope, multimeter, current clamp and video scope.
