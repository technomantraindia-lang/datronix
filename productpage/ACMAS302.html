AC Service Station ACMAS302

Entry Model for Automotive A/C Service

Entry Model, Workshop Ready

DaTo ACMAS302 is an entry model A/C service station, suitable for new A/C practitioners and price-sensitive areas. It supports key refrigerant service workflows including tank fill, recovery, vacuum, oil injection, charge and fully automatic operation.

  • Tank fill, recovery, vacuum, oil injection and charge functions
  • Fully automatic service mode for daily A/C workflows
  • Heater band and hand valves free operation
  • Amply database with free update support
  • Highly integrated pipeline manifold
  • Condenser and cooling fan included
  • Automatic service reminding across 600 operations

Description

Product Overview

  • Entry model suitable for new A/C practitioners and price-sensitive markets.
  • Main functions include tank fill, recovery, vacuum, oil injection, charge and fully automatic mode.
  • Optional functions include flush, compressor oil recovery and vacuum leak test.
  • Optional 120 L/min vacuum pump, UV dye injection and HFO-1234yf support.
  • Heater band, hand valves free design and integrated pipeline manifold.
  • Condenser and cooling fan included for stable operation.
  • Pressurization helps speed up used oil discharge.
  • Automatic service reminder warns at 480 operations and locks after 600 operations until service is done.
